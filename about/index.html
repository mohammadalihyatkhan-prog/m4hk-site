Statement

I tell stories by painting, by drawing and by cutting prints.

Mostly I tell what is left of them. The plot goes first. What stays is someone caught mid-moment, a companion or two, a name, a line from a song. I keep pulling the painting towards a drawing, and I still love a bold, fluid brushstroke.

Since childhood I’ve built worlds: invented scripts, borrowed mythologies, stories about stories. The scripts were never readable. Nothing I built was meant to hold.

I live and work in Lahore.

Biography

M4HK (Mohammad Ali Hyat Khan, b. 1989, Lahore) is a self-taught artist based in Lahore. He paints, draws and makes relief prints about what is left of stories: figures, names and gestures whose plots have gone. He has exhibited in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad since 2021.
