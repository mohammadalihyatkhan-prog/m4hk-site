Statement

I tell stories by painting, by drawing and by cutting prints.

Mostly I tell what is left of them. The plot goes first. What stays is someone caught mid-moment, a companion or two, a name, a line from a song. I keep pulling the painting towards a drawing, and I still love a bold, fluid brushstroke.

Since childhood I’ve built worlds: invented scripts, borrowed mythologies, stories about stories. The scripts were never readable. Nothing I built was meant to hold.

Biography

M4HK (Mohammad Ali Hyat Khan, b. 1989, Lahore) is a self-taught artist working in painting, drawing and relief print. Since 2021 he has exhibited in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad, in group exhibitions and in two-person exhibitions with the artist Ujala Khan, with whom he also makes joint work. In 2025 their joint work was included in Seeing, س (Seen) at the Pakistan National Council of the Arts, Islamabad. His work is in the permanent collection of Ambiance Boutique Art Hotels. He lives and works in Lahore.
